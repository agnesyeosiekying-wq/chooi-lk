Eyelid Surgery in Malaysia

Blepharoplasty is a specialised procedure to improve the appearance of the upper and lower eyelids. It addresses drooping skin, puffiness, and eye bags, helping patients look more refreshed and youthful.

Types of Blepharoplasty

Upper Blepharoplasty

Removes excess skin and fat from the upper eyelids to improve droopiness and restore a more alert, youthful appearance. In some cases, it may also improve visual field obstruction.

Lower Blepharoplasty

Targets eye bags, puffiness, and under-eye shadows. Techniques may include fat repositioning or removal to achieve a smoother and more refreshed look.

Combined Approach

In many patients, both upper and lower eyelids are treated together to achieve optimal balance and overall facial rejuvenation.

What Eyelid Surgery Can Address

Droopy Upper Eyelids

Excess skin causing a tired or heavy appearance.

Eye Bags

Puffiness under the eyes creating a fatigued look.

Loose Skin

Age-related skin laxity around the eyes.

Hollow Under-Eye

Volume loss leading to shadowing and dark circles.
