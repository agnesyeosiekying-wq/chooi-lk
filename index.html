About Dr Chooi Lai Kuan

Dr Chooi Lai Kuan is a Consultant Plastic and Reconstructive Surgeon, known for combining surgical precision with refined, natural aesthetic outcomes.

She previously served as Head of the Plastic Surgery Department at Hospital Sungai Buloh, a major burn referral centre in Malaysia, where she managed complex cases involving burn injuries, scar reconstruction, and soft tissue defects.

She further underwent fellowship training at Chang Gung Memorial Hospital, Taiwan, a renowned centre for plastic and reconstructive surgery, where she gained advanced exposure to facial aesthetic surgery and rhinoplasty.

Dr Chooi holds a Master of Surgery (Plastic Surgery) from Universiti Sains Malaysia and is an active member of MAPACS, MSPRS, and the Rhinoplasty Society of Asia.

Her approach to facial aesthetics is grounded in a deep understanding of facial anatomy and surgical principles, allowing her to deliver balanced, natural results while maintaining the highest standards of safety.
