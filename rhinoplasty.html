Rhinoplasty (Nose Surgery)

Rhinoplasty in Malaysia

Rhinoplasty is a surgical procedure to reshape the nose for improved facial balance and proportion. It may also address breathing difficulties and structural concerns.

Dr Chooi’s Approach

Dr Chooi Lai Kuan approaches rhinoplasty with a focus on achieving natural and harmonious outcomes tailored to each patient’s facial structure.

With fellowship training at Chang Gung Memorial Hospital, Taiwan, she integrates advanced techniques with strong reconstructive principles to ensure both aesthetic refinement and functional preservation.
