Rhinoplasty (Nose Surgery)

Advanced nasal reshaping surgery designed to enhance facial harmony while maintaining natural features and function.

Rhinoplasty in Malaysia

Rhinoplasty is a surgical procedure to reshape the nose for improved facial balance and proportion. It may also address breathing difficulties and structural concerns.

Comprehensive Rhinoplasty Techniques

Rhinoplasty is a highly specialised procedure that combines both aesthetic refinement and structural reconstruction. Each surgery is tailored to the individual, taking into account facial proportions, skin thickness, and anatomical considerations.

Silicone Implant Rhinoplasty

A commonly used technique in Asian rhinoplasty to enhance nasal bridge height with predictable and refined contours.

Autologous Rib Cartilage

Ideal for more complex cases, providing strong structural support and long-term stability using the patient’s own tissue.

Dermal Fat Grafting

A natural option for soft augmentation, suitable for subtle contouring and improving skin quality.

Precision Refinement Techniques

In addition to structural augmentation, rhinoplasty often involves detailed refinement techniques to achieve balance and harmony.

Alar Base Reduction

Refining nostril width to create a more proportionate nasal base.

Tip Plasty

Enhancing nasal tip definition, projection, and symmetry.

Customised Combination Approach

Combining multiple techniques when required to achieve optimal aesthetic and functional outcomes.
